## The brief

After having attended a culture evening program in the consulate in June of 2026, I was enamored by the consulate; entering it felt like stepping back in time, and it was as if time had froze there since the late 90's or the early 2000's. The carpets on the floor, the quaint bookshelves and the tick-tock of those clocks on the wall spoke for themselves.

Having been a student of the Japanese language for almost a year at that point, I found my cultural knowledge, interaction with the consulate and profeciency in Graphic Design all at an intersection. That intersection blossomed into a booklet, 44-pages long, studying the history of the Consulate.

## The design

The booklet runs in eight chapters: the consulate's founding, its place in Jeddah, the consuls-general, cultural exchange, its associate organisations, where it's heading, a photo gallery, and contacts. The palette pairs washi paper tones with navy, red and sakura pink, with ensō brush circles as a recurring motif. It's typeset in Garamond, New Tegomin and Times New Roman, and laid out in Affinity Publisher.
