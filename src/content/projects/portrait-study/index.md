## The idea

Having completed a year as a self-taught student of fine art and illustration, I picked up my copy of Loomis' guide to drawing the head and hands and illustrated the lady from the cover. This illustration would become the centerpiece of one of my first prints.

## Process

Working mostly in digital graphite brushes in CSP, I hoped to keep this sketch true to its traditional aspects, though it is a digital illustration made in CSP.
