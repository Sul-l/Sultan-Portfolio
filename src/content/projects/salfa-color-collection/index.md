## The brief

Salfa's Color Collection gives each fragrance its own colour, from emerald and sapphire to rose and sunflower gold. The posts needed to sell the collection as a set while giving every scent a moment of its own.

## The work

I built the series around two kinds of image. Set shots show the whole collection in its gift box, styled on a desk of handwritten paper so the label colours stand out against a quiet ground. Single-scent posts give each fragrance its own world: Golden Sunflower sits in a swirl of gold and amber, with the Salfa wordmark and the scent name set above it.

Each image was styled and shot, then retouched and composited, with the type set to match the brand's labels.
