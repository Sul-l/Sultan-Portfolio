## The idea

Cladia, a young socialite who works for a cult encroaching on her city. She targets people in high places, removing them and paving the way for the city's future assailants. 

Inspired by games like Bioshock Infinite and set in the regency period, I aimed to build a loose character concept; portrait detail and a full portrait, with a basic background.

## Process

Illustrated in CSP, I used pastel-like and oil brushes to get the style down right. I kept everything deliberately loose and messy, and in typical style left some of the pallete on screen, paving the way for a future development of this concept.

## Outcome

This piece is now one of my prized digital artworks and has its place in my portfolio as a study piece. It is a small sample of what I could output as a digital illustrator.
