## The idea

The Sailor Fude de Mannen has a bent nib, so the line it draws changes with the angle you hold it at. The campaign makes that its whole message: one pen, every kind of mark, from a hairline to a block of ink.

## The work

Two A-series posters lead the campaign. "One Nib. A Thousand Voices." works like a specimen sheet, labelling every mark the pen can make. "Bent Not Broken" is the image-led version, with a vertical line of Japanese running down the side. Two 4:5 posts carry the same idea onto social, cut down to a few words each.

## Process

Sourcing every asset in this campaign through my own work, I used my own sketches and lettering that I made using this pen as props and backgrounds for each photo. Every background, page or artwork here is a real existing artwork that I have made using the star of the show; the Sailor Fude de Mannen Fountain Pen. In this I hoped to be entirely self-reliant with a product; to display its use-cases authentically, to do the product photography and to design the flyers and banners for it. 

Photos taken on my desk. Designs made using Affinity.
