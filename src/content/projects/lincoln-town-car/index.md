## The idea

The old D body model Lincoln Town Cars are to me some of the most aesthetically pleasing cars, and symbols of old luxury in automobiles. I created this design to experiment with digonal designs in affinity, and experiment with some things.

## The design

The diagonal split runs through the type as well as the background, so TOWN CAR reads white on navy and black on cream. Copper line icons along the bottom pick out the car's selling points, and the tagline closes the poster in widely spaced capitals.
