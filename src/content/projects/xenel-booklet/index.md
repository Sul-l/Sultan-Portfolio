## The idea

Rather than send a standard CV, I made a short booklet that tells Xenel's story, from its founding in Jeddah in 1973 to the group of companies it is today, and shows where I would fit into it.

## The design

Headings are set in Palatino Linotype, section labels in Playfair Display and body text in Garamond, on an ivory and warm-paper palette with Xenel black. The booklet documents its own design system on a "Behind This Design" page. I took the photos of Xenel Tower on my phone, and the other photos came from the Saudi Photos Hub. It was laid out in Affinity Publisher.
