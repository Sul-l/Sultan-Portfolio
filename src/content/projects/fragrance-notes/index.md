## The brief

During my client work with Salfa, I was given the request of redesigning their banner, with the most basic and vague instructions possible. In carrying out this task I rebuilt the banner but also made a full brand study deck for them, critiquing their existing brand direction and lack of direction and giving my input on what could be fixed. For their new banner I hand-illustrated a fine art piece in a single day in CSP, with still life perfume ingredients, and used this art piece to create both horizontal and vertical banners for their website with two select perfumes.

## Process

For their new banner I hand-illustrated a fine art piece in a single day in CSP, with still life perfume ingredients, and used this art piece to create both horizontal and vertical banners for their website with two select perfumes. Work was done in CSP and Adobe Photoshop.

## Outcome

This illustration was the centerpiece for my deck submission to Salfa, and the marketing lead in the brand was pleased with it.
