## The idea

A few hand-illustrated panels, part of a storyboard for a short story I am working on.

## Process

Originally starting as a digital study with sepia colors, textured brushes and ballpoint brushes, I further developed this study into a short storyboard in Clip Studio Paint. The broad brushstrokes in the background sometimes convey an abstract mood or concept. More red conveys sanguinity.
